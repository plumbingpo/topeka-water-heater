Safe Installation

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

A poorly installed or neglected water heater can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in tanks stored too cool, and expensive water damage when a tank fails. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

Maintenance Programs

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Topeka Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water hardness, usage patterns, and budget.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints.
